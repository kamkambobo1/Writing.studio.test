Unbound

Becoming

With unshakable conviction, he marched towards his dreams, Like a river flowing relentlessly to the ocean. He flowed towards his aspirations A statue of liberty incarnate he embodied freedom from self-regret His book, pen and his hands stuck by Cupid's arrow. Chartering his course, navigating life's obstacles like Odysseus through the sirens. With the hope of emerging from his struggles, like a phoenix from the ashes. Bright lights blazed and innovative ideas brewed. Bright beams beckoned, brilliant brightness burst forth, And bright ideas blossomed, illuminating his path, His path to greatness --- Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu
