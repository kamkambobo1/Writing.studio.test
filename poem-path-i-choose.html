The Path I Choose

Becoming

Promise yourself you’ll find love. Enjoy yourself. Be happy, and choose your own future. Focus on your aspirations, not people’s expectations — do it only if it’s truly what you want. Be Strong, even when it’s not easy. Be Optimistic, no matter the situation. Be Visionary — see beyond today. Be Ambitious — never settle for less. Be Tenacious — keep pushing forward. Be Authentic — never lose yourself ✨ Always Maintain your cool. Always Yearn for the best — crave growth. Never Limit yourself or let fear lead. Never Obey doubt or overlook your worth. Never Vanish in the crowd or question your value. Never Erase your dreams or give up, even when it’s hard. --- Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu
