About the Poet

Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu

Writer. Poet. Observer of the quiet spaces between heartbeats.

Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu Emmanuel O. is a Nigerian poet and creative who hails from Anambra State. He is the fourth-born in his family and the first male, with an introverted personality and a natural preference for solitude and the things that genuinely capture his interest.

His journey with poetry began long before he fully understood it. Although he had studied Literature from JSS1, it was during SS1, under the teaching of Mrs. Carol Alimi, that poetry began to make sense to him. Her detailed explanations and determination to make her students understand gradually shaped his understanding of poetry, even before he consciously realized he was learning.

His first documented poem, Beyond the Blank Page, marked an important point in his writing journey. Other early works included Beyond the Thorns, Wisdom's Light, and Unbound. In those early days, he was more concerned with rhyme and making words fit together. With time, however, his writing began to change. He became less interested in forcing rhythm or using complicated language and more interested in expressing what he genuinely felt.

Today, he does not force himself to write. A poem may come to him unexpectedly, sometimes leading to several pieces in a day, while at other times weeks or even a month may pass before another poem takes shape. He often writes on his phone, sometimes beginning with a simple phrase, a thought, a conversation, or an experience and allowing it to grow naturally.

Much of his poetry is inspired by love, personal experiences, and struggles. Yet he does not write with the expectation that every reader will understand a poem in the same way. One person may find a poem deeply relatable, another may simply appreciate its beauty, while someone else may find a completely different meaning—or none at all. For him, that difference is part of what makes poetry interesting.

Beyond poetry, he enjoys drawing, particularly anime-inspired art, gaming, action movies, and digital creativity. He is also interested in developing his ability to turn ordinary things into meaningful pieces of art and to express his thoughts and emotions with greater clarity.

He does not consider himself a finished poet. He sees himself as someone still learning, experimenting, and discovering what he can do with words.

He writes simply because sometimes, the words come—and when they do, he lets them speak.
