Her Pull

Silence

Her pull of my heart, a stitch in the soul. Heartbeat borrowed a map from your smile, ran miles it didn’t understand, got lost on purpose— just to keep asking a name in places that only exist in imagination. Eyes picked up a habit— finding your presence in every crowded room. Magnetic… hard to look away from. Phone forgot how to sleep, waiting for a goodnight. Whole nights lean on one message. The future learned how to rewrite itself around wherever you stand. Every plan made now… somehow has you in it. A worst day can be heavy, loud, overwhelming— then you show up, and suddenly problems start acting shy. --- Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu
