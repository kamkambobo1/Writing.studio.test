Storm in Disguise

Struggle & Wonder

For every action There’s a reaction. Excess appraisals — Sycophantic praises, Always caring, yet secretly sneering. Open smiles, hidden anger. A raging storm Behind a calm appearance. Waiting for the perfect fault — Just one day, Only an hour, A minute, Within seconds… And boom. The ultimate outburst Of pervasive, raging anger. Evil accusations — What an aberration. A fake smile — It had been there all along, Silently nursing hatred, Tending resentment, Secretly harbouring deceit. But tell me — Why not attack the inactions? Why not correct the mistakes? Why not attract attention With good intentions? Why wait for unruly plight? Why wait for a fight? Why not resolve the issue in the light Instead of waiting for night? Why not correct the wrong And make it right, Knowing fully well That two wrongs can’t make a right? Why bear the pain When there is no gain? Why not be frank and plain Instead of waiting in vain For history To repeat itself again? For every action there's a reaction For every reaction there's an action --- Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu
