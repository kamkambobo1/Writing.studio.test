The Uncertainty Within

A reflection on emotions, confusion, and the need for reassurance.

Collection: Struggle & Wonder

Why? Why can't I control my emotions? Why? Why can't I undo all I've done? Why do you control me? I wish I could be free— free from your grasp, your manipulations. I'm tired. Is it a habit, or is this an obsession? I'm weak. I don't believe it. Is there really a connection? I'm confused. Will I be okay? I need to talk, but I'm always short of words. Does your presence scare me? Is it normal, or am I weird? Is this okay? I think I'm scared. I was never ready for it. I never asked for it. It wasn't planned. I don't think I regret it, but please help me. I'm confused. I hope I'm not wrong, that I'm not fooling myself. I don't want to make mistakes. I need reassurance. I need a sign that I'm not making a fool of myself.
