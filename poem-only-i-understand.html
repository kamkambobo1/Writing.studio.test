Only I Understand

Silence

I want to tell someone— I have people I think I trust. But after talking to myself, after planning it all in my head, I just feel like I don’t want to disturb them. I just want to be. Because only I understand what I see, what happens to me, what it feels like to carry it quietly. And maybe that is my flaw— building walls out of silence, calling it strength. There are words I keep folding like letters I never send, thinking quiet will protect us both. If my silence ever sounded like distance, if my quiet ever felt like indifference, it was never meant to wound. Some apologies don’t shout. They sit softly between lines, hoping to be understood. Because even when I say nothing, I am still feeling everything. --- Ibeakuzie Kamsiriochukwu
